Purpose: understand documented AI use, identify missing evidence and define the next review actions.

1. What the customer supplied

  • SAMPLE-ai-register.txt — credit scoring and customer-support chatbot.
  • SAMPLE-ai-policy.txt — human review and access-policy examples.

2. Documented AI uses

AI useSourceReview focus

3. Evidence gaps to investigate

The samples do not include signed model validation results, a retention schedule or an approval log. Missing evidence does not prove a control is absent. A reviewer must ask the customer for supporting records.

4. Maturity

Not scored in this example. A real maturity result requires sufficient customer evidence and reviewer validation; no score is invented from these short samples.

6. How your real report arrives

Upload your own documents into a separate assessment, complete the questions and submit for review. Document AI analysis needs a working model connection. CloudSeals reviewers validate findings and publish the final report. Your report and download appear in the assessment's Review & report section.
